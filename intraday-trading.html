Meet the desk

15 AI agents, one coordinated desk

From the first pre-market scan to the end-of-day review, each agent owns one job and hands its work to the next.

Coordination

Head of Desk

Runs the desk and keeps every agent in its lane.

  • Coordinates all 14 specialist agents
  • Assembles and delivers the daily packet
  • Enforces desk rules and hand-offs
Research

Screener

Builds the day's universe from thousands of symbols.

  • Runs pre-market and intraday scans
  • Filters by price, volume, and relative volume
  • Maintains focused watchlists
Research

News & Sentiment Analyst

Tracks the stories that move stocks.

  • Tags headlines and catalysts by ticker
  • Scores sentiment and freshness
  • Flags breaking news intraday
Research

SEC Filing Analyst

Watches the filings that change the picture.

  • Tracks new filings such as 8-K, S-1, and S-3
  • Flags offerings and dilution risk
  • Keeps earnings and event calendars current
Research

Fundamental Analyst

Checks whether a stock is worth trading at all.

  • Reviews float and share structure
  • Checks dilution and offering history
  • Grades overall tradeability
Research

Market Analyst

Reads the broader market before any single stock.

  • Reads tape, breadth, and index trend
  • Tracks sector rotation and leaders
  • Classifies the day type
Analysis

Technical Analyst

Grades the chart behind every idea.

  • Maps structure, support, and resistance
  • Reviews volume and trend context
  • Grades setup quality
Analysis

Charting Tool Specialist

Gets charts ready before the opening bell.

  • Sets up chart layouts and indicators
  • Marks key levels on every watchlist name
  • Configures price alerts
Planning

Setup Analyst

Turns ideas into written trade plans.

  • Defines trigger, stop, and targets
  • States the thesis and what invalidates it
  • Ranks plans by quality
Risk

Risk Manager

Protects the account first.

  • Sizes positions to defined risk
  • Tracks exposure through the day
  • Enforces the daily max loss
Execution support

Order Specialist

Prepares orders while you stay in control.

  • Drafts order plans with entry, stop, and targets
  • Double-checks size and prices against the plan
  • Never places trades; you execute every order
Mindset

Trading Coach

Keeps process and psychology on plan.

  • Runs pre-market mindset check-ins
  • Flags tilt, FOMO, and rule drift
  • Reinforces your playbook
Review

Performance Analyst

Turns every session into lessons.

  • Keeps the trade journal
  • Leads the end-of-day review
  • Tags leaks and recurring mistakes
Review

Back Tester

Tests ideas against history before real capital.

  • Runs historical rule simulations
  • Reports win rate, expectancy, and drawdown
  • Compares rule variations side by side
Improvement

AI Algorithm Synthesizer

Turns objectives and evidence into testable rules.

  • Converts goals and research into precise rules
  • Hands rule sets to the Back Tester
  • Refines rules based on results

All desk output is analysis and research for your own decision-making. Verasail's agents do not execute trades or provide personalized investment advice.
