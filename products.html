Service management

Proactive Service Desk Agent

Fix it before anyone files a ticket. The Service Desk Agent watches systems, telemetry, and user signals for the early signs and symptoms of trouble, then triages, analyzes, and resolves issues before they turn into outages or complaints. Configurable and non-configurable changes are validated with built-in test automation. The service ticket becomes an afterthought: it's created automatically after the fact, purely to document the item for trend analysis.

  • Early detection of signs and symptoms, before a ticket exists
  • Automatic triage, prioritization, and root-cause analysis
  • Configurable and non-configurable changes, validated by test automation
  • Tickets and defects auto-logged afterward for trend analysis
