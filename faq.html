FAQ

Frequently asked questions about agentic AI and AI agents

Short answers about agentic AI workforces, AI agents, orchestration, compliance monitoring, the proactive service desk, retraining and transformation. Each links to a fuller article.

What is an agentic AI workforce?

It is a coordinated team of AI agents, each with a defined role, that work toward goals, use tools and data and hand off to people when judgment is needed. See what is an agentic AI workforce.

What is the difference between an AI assistant and an AI agent?

An assistant responds to prompts. An agent pursues a goal: it breaks work into steps, chooses tools, takes actions, checks results and asks for help when needed.

What do AI agents for product management do?

They draft user stories and acceptance criteria, support backlog refinement and prioritization, and prepare release notes and stakeholder summaries, while product owners make the decisions. See AI agents for product management.

What do AI agents for portfolio management do?

They consolidate status across initiatives, surface dependencies and risks, show capacity and alignment, and answer questions about the portfolio. See AI agents for portfolio management.

What is AI orchestration?

It is the coordination layer that routes work between agents and people, sequences multi-step workflows, handles approvals and failures, and keeps activity visible. See AI orchestration hub explained.

What is AI compliance monitoring?

It uses agents to check processes, data and activity against your policies and controls continuously, raise exceptions and keep audit-ready evidence, covering both people and AI agents. See AI compliance monitoring.

What is a proactive AI service desk agent?

It watches for early signs and symptoms of trouble, triages and resolves issues before a ticket exists, and logs the ticket afterward for trend analysis. See the proactive AI service desk.

Why retrain knowledge workers for AI?

People who can direct AI agents, review their output and design workflows get more value from them and spend more time on judgment-based work. See retraining knowledge workers.

How should we start an AI transformation?

Clarify outcomes, assess processes and data, prioritize a few use cases, prepare governance and people, pilot, then scale in stages. See the AI transformation roadmap.

Does Verasail offer custom AI agent development?

Yes. We help organizations build custom AI agents on their own ecosystem and data. See Custom AI Agent Development.

Can we buy ready-made agents?

Yes. Verasail offers ready-made agents and platform products such as the Orchestration Hub and Compliance Monitoring. See products.

Do AI agents replace people?

Our approach is to move repetitive, data-heavy work to agents so people can focus on decisions, relationships and creative work, with people overseeing the agents. Roles will change, which is why we pair deployment with retraining.

How do you handle data governance?

Data governance and quality are part of how we work: ownership, standards and automated checks so agents rely on trustworthy data. See Data Governance & Quality.
