Enable

AI Workforce Retraining

Help your people make the shift from menial, manual work to meaningful, context-driven work. We retrain knowledge workers to orchestrate AI agents, not compete with them, so your teams direct the work, apply judgment, and capture the full value of your AI investment. The program runs step by step, with 1:1 working sessions on real work that keep progress moving until the benefits are fully realized.

  • Structured training program for leadership, teams, and individuals
  • Step-by-step curriculum for orchestrating AI agents
  • 1:1 working sessions on real work to drive progress
  • 1:1 coaching for leaders and key contributors
  • Private, role-based cohorts for your company
